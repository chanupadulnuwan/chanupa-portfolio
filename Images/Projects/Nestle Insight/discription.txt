🚀 Proud to share one of the most valuable academic projects I've completed so far.

As part of our Level 5 Commercial Computing module, our team developed Nestlé Insight — a mobile and web-based demand-signal platform designed to improve visibility in traditional retail supply chains.

Many small retailers operate without POS systems, making it difficult to capture real sales and demand signals accurately. This can result in poor forecasting, stockouts, and overstocking.

📱 Mobile App
Designed for Sales Representatives, Shop Owners, and Distributors to manage orders, deliveries, outlet registration, route planning, returns, field reports, and shop-level data collection.

🌐 Web Portal
Designed for Admins, Warehouse Managers, and Demand Planners to manage users, monitor operations, access dashboards, reports, forecasting tools, and demand-planning exports.

📊 A key feature was our Demand Forecast Export Generator, which transformed shop-level operational data into forecasting-ready exports such as sales, inventory, orders, and master data—helping convert traditional retail activity into valuable demand signals.

⚙️ Tech Stack
Flutter • React • NestJS • PostgreSQL/MySQL • Figma • GitHub • Jira

🧩 My Role
I served as the project lead/coordinator while contributing across multiple Agile roles, including Developer, Business Analyst, QA Support, and Sprint Coordination. I was involved in planning, system design, documentation, development support, testing, and presentations.

✅ Key Learning
This project strengthened my understanding of Agile practices, teamwork, leadership, communication, and how technology can solve real business challenges.
